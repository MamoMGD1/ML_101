# Section02 — Unsupervised Learning  

## 📝 Section Review  

In the real world, most data comes **without labels** — there’s no teacher, no answers, just patterns waiting to be discovered.  
That’s where **Unsupervised Learning** steps in: instead of predicting known outcomes, we explore the hidden structure of the data itself.  

By the end of this section, you’ll understand how unsupervised models **learn from raw experience**, uncovering organization and meaning in ways that even humans sometimes miss.  

---

## 0️⃣ Clustering  

Clustering is the art of discovering **natural groupings** in unlabeled data.  
From customer segmentation to document categorization, clustering algorithms help reveal structure by grouping similar observations together.  
In this chapter, you’ll learn how algorithms like *K-Means* and *DBSCAN* separate chaos into clarity — giving shape to the hidden order of your data.

## 1️⃣ Dimensionality Reduction  

High-dimensional data can be overwhelming — not only for humans but also for algorithms.  
**Dimensionality Reduction** simplifies this complexity, projecting data into lower dimensions while preserving as much information as possible.  
In this chapter, you’ll explore how techniques like *PCA*, *t-SNE*, and *UMAP* make visualization, computation, and pattern discovery easier and more insightful.
