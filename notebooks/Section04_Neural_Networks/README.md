# Section04 — Neural Networks  

## 📝 Section Review  

Neural Networks are the backbone of modern machine learning, enabling computers to **learn complex patterns and representations** from data.  
This section introduces the fundamental components of neural networks — from neurons and layers to training algorithms — and demonstrates how machines can approximate almost any function given enough data and proper architecture.  

Understanding neural networks is essential for progressing into deep learning, computer vision, natural language processing, and other advanced AI domains.

## 0️⃣ Neural Networks  

This chapter covers the essentials of neural networks:  
- Structure: neurons, layers, and network architectures  
- Forward propagation and making predictions  
- Loss functions and optimization techniques  
- Backpropagation and gradient descent  
- Training and evaluating neural networks  

Through hands-on code implementations, you’ll gain a practical understanding of how neural networks learn and generalize from data.
